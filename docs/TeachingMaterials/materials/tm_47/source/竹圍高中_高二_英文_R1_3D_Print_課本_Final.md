## ④文意理解

Claude 認為：
標題 Imagination Is the Only Limit。 3D 列印讓「做不到」的界線幾乎消失，唯一的限制只剩人的想像力。

P1 的轉折。 「Many might consider it ridiculous... But with 3D printing... it's already a reality.」前句寫一般人的懷疑，後句以 But 翻轉，說明夢想已經實現，奠定全文論點。

P2 的關鍵理解。

it seemed that his life would never be like that of other children：表示「看似」如此，是推測語氣，後文的 However 推翻了這個預期。
that of other children 中的 that 代替單數名詞 life，即 the life of other children。
which promises to benefit people like Alex 中的 which 指前面整句 artificial limbs can be produced relatively cheaply，也就是「低價生產義肢」這件事帶來希望。
in ways that they never dreamed possible 表示以他們從未夢想過的方式。

Q1. The new robotic arm is capable of ______ complex surgical procedures with precision.
(A) perform　(B) performing　(C) performed　(D) to perform
答案：B
詳解：be capable of 的 of 為介系詞，後接名詞或動名詞，故選 performing。

Q2. Many people consider ______ important to set goals for their personal and professional lives.
(A) that　(B) this　(C) it　(D) them
答案：C
詳解：句型為 S + consider + it + adj. + to VR，it 是虛受詞，真正的受詞是後面的不定詞片語 to set goals...。

Q3. The mortgage rate at this bank is lower than ______ of the other bank.
(A) it　(B) those　(C) one　(D) that
答案：D
詳解：比較對象是單數名詞 the mortgage rate，用指示代名詞 that 代替。代替複數名詞才用 those。

Q4. AI tools can instantly carry out complex tasks, ______ enables people to work more efficiently.
(A) that　(B) which　(C) what　(D) it
答案：B
詳解：逗號後的非限定用法關係代名詞只能用 which，此處 which 指前面整句。that 不能用於非限定用法，what 不引導形容詞子句，it 會造成兩句無連接詞的錯誤。

Q5. The sensor in the plastic electronic arm would enable Alex ______ his hand by slightly flexing his biceps muscle.
(A) open　(B) opening　(C) to open　(D) opened
答案：C
詳解：enable + O + to VR 意為「使……能夠……」，故選 to open。

Q6. In the subtitle "Body Parts That Don't Cost an Arm and a Leg," what does the phrase mean?
(A) The body parts are not very expensive.
(B) The body parts cannot replace real arms and legs.
(C) The body parts are made without plastic.
(D) The body parts are only for children.
答案：A
詳解：cost an arm and a leg 意為「非常昂貴」，加上否定即「不貴」。這也呼應課文 artificial limbs can be produced relatively cheaply，同時與義肢主題形成雙關。

Q7. According to the passage, why have many sea creatures suffered?
(A) Because 3D-printed reefs attract too many fish.
(B) Because the Caribbean project was launched too late.
(C) Because coral reefs are too rough in texture.
(D) Because of the decreasing number of coral reefs caused by rising sea temperatures.
答案：D
詳解：P3 指出全球暖化使海溫上升，海洋生物因此受害，尤其是因為珊瑚礁數量減少（especially due to the decreasing number of coral reefs）。

Q8. Sea temperatures have risen ______ an alarming degree because of global warming.
(A) in　(B) at　(C) to　(D) for
答案：C
詳解：to a(n) + adj. + degree/extent 意為「到……的程度」，故選 to。

Q9. To help protect sea creatures' habitats, a project ______ on 3D printing has been launched.
(A) relies　(B) relying　(C) relied　(D) is relying
答案：B
詳解：句中已有主要動詞 has been launched，此處需用分詞片語修飾 project。由 which relies on 省略關代並改為現在分詞 relying，表主動。

## ⑫易錯陷阱

Claude 認為：
把 imagine 接 to V。 ✗ couldn't imagine to live abroad → ✓ imagine living abroad
be capable of 接原形動詞。 ✗ is capable of adapt → ✓ is capable of adapting
漏掉虛受詞 it。 ✗ consider important to set goals → ✓ consider it important to set goals
非限定子句用 that。 ✗ ..., that enables people... → ✓ ..., which enables people...；逗號也不可省略。
比較對象不對等。 ✗ The rate at this bank is lower than the other bank. → ✓ ...than that of the other bank.
enable 漏掉 to。 ✗ enable users learn → ✓ enable users to learn
because of 與 because 混用。 ✗ because global warming → ✓ because of global warming
雙重動詞。 ✗ a project relies on 3D printing has been launched → ✓ a project relying on 3D printing has been launched
數字混淆。 17 天是列印時間，2 天是組裝時間，250 是平方公尺。閱讀題常以此設陷阱。
慣用語照字面理解。 cost an arm and a leg 不是「付出一條手臂和一條腿」，而是「非常昂貴」。
SOURCE 文字問題提醒。 SOURCE P2 作 "engineering student"，但圖像筆記作 "Engineering students"，且動詞 collaborated（合作）語意上需複數主詞，應為 students，請以紙本為準。
