30 題標準單選練習題（題號 Q1～Q30，包含完整題幹、四選一選項、答案與詳解）
Q1. Modern smart appliances are now ______ performing multiple domestic chores simultaneously based on user voice commands.

(A) able of (B) capable of (C) potential to (D) skilled with

答案：B

詳解：句型考查。"be capable of + V-ing" 為固定搭配，表「有能力做某事」；若使用 able 則需搭配不定詞 "able to perform"；potential 後不接 of V-ing。故選 (B)。

Q2. When smartphones were first introduced decades ago, many industry critics considered ______ absurd to imagine carrying a pocket computer everywhere.

(A) this (B) that (C) it (D) what

答案：C

詳解：文法虛受詞考查。"S + consider + it + adj. + to VR" 句型中，真正受詞為後方的 "to imagine carrying a pocket computer"，受詞位置需置入虛受詞 "it"。故選 (C)。

Q3. Students are strongly encouraged to ______ the comprehensive academic databases available at the university library to support their research.

(A) make use of (B) keep track of (C) lose sight of (D) run out of

答案：A

詳解：片語考查。(A) 善用、利用；(B) 追蹤、記錄；(C) 忽視、忘卻；(D) 用盡。句意指鼓勵學生善用大學圖書館的學術資料庫，故選 (A)。

Q4. The climate research team warned that global atmospheric temperatures have risen to a(n) ______ extent over the past three decades.

(A) relative (B) individual (C) alarming (D) electronic

答案：C

詳解：字彙題。(A) 相對的；(B) 個別的；(C) 令人擔憂的、驚人的；(D) 電子的。搭配 "to an alarming extent/degree" 表「達到令人警惕的嚴重程度」，故選 (C)。

Q5. In the international robotics contest, high school students from three different nations collaborated to ______ the engineering challenge.

(A) overcome (B) launch (C) flex (D) assemble

答案：A

詳解：詞彙與文意考查。(A) 克服；(B) 發起；(C) 收縮、彎曲；(D) 組裝。與 challenge（挑戰）最合適的動詞搭配為 overcome（克服），collaborate to overcome 表合作克服難關，故選 (A)。

Q6. The boy was fitted with an innovative bionic arm equipped with an advanced electronic ______, which responds immediately to minor muscle twitches.

(A) muscle (B) sensor (C) habitat (D) texture

答案：B

詳解：單字題。(A) 肌肉；(B) 感應器、感測器；(C) 棲息地；(D) 質地。能對肌肉收縮做出反應並傳導電子訊號的裝置為 sensor，故選 (B)。

Q7. By ______ his shoulder and upper arm muscles slightly, the young patient learned how to open and close his artificial prosthetic hand.

(A) flexing (B) assembling (C) launching (D) dreaming

答案：A

詳解：單字題。(A) 收縮（肌肉）、彎曲（關節）；(B) 組裝；(C) 發動、發射；(D) 做夢。句意指藉由稍微收縮肌肉來控制義手開合，故選 (A)。

Q8. Compared with conventional handmade prosthetics, 3D-printed limbs can be produced ______ cheaply, making them accessible to underprivileged families.

(A) ridiculously (B) consistently (C) relatively (D) dangerously

答案：C

詳解：副詞考查。(A) 荒謬地；(B) 一貫地；(C) 相對地；(D) 危險地。課文強調 3D 列印技術使義肢生產成本「相對低廉（relatively cheaply）」，故選 (C)。

Q9. The performance of the revised public transit system is noticeably superior to ______ of the old one in terms of punctuality.

(A) one (B) it (C) that (D) those

答案：C

詳解：指示代名詞考查。此處用於指代前文出現過的單數名詞 performance（表現），為避免重複且具有特定後位修飾，必須使用 "that"；若為複數名詞則使用 "those"。故選 (C)。

Q10. The cutting-edge medical software enables junior doctors ______ complex surgical simulations without exposing actual patients to risks.

(A) practice (B) practicing (C) to practice (D) practiced

答案：C

詳解：動詞句型考查。"enable + O + to VR" 為固定句型，表示「使……能夠做……」，後方受詞補語必須接帶 to 的不定詞，故選 (C)。

Q11. The university laboratory succeeded in developing a brand-new composite material, ______ paved the way for lighter and stronger aircraft wings.

(A) which (B) that (C) what (D) who

答案：A

詳解：關係代名詞考查。逗號後方的關係子句修飾前方「實驗室成功研發全新複合材料」整件事，非限定子句中代替整件事的關代只能使用 which，不可使用 that。故選 (A)。

Q12. Thousands of delicate marine organisms along the coastline lost their natural ______ due to extensive chemical pollution from nearby factories.

(A) concepts (B) habitats (C) materials (D) structures

答案：B

詳解：單字題。(A) 概念；(B) 棲息地；(C) 材料；(D) 結構。marine organisms（海洋生物）失去的是天然棲地，故選 (B)。

Q13. To tackle the rising issue of urban plastic waste, the local municipal council has recently ______ a comprehensive zero-waste campaign.

(A) launched (B) flexed (C) assembled (D) cost

答案：A

詳解：詞彙搭配考查。(A) 發起、推展；(B) 收縮；(C) 裝配；(D) 花費。campaign（倡議、活動）常用的搭配動詞為 launch，表發起一項運動，故選 (A)。

Q14. The man-made restoration structure was engineered to mimic native coral beds not only in texture ______ in geometric complexity.

(A) as well as (B) but also (C) along with (D) instead of

答案：B

詳解：對等連接詞考查。"not only A but also B" 為固定對等結構，表「不僅 A 而且 B」，連接對稱的介系詞片語 in texture 與 in geometric complexity，故選 (B)。

Q15. If ecological restoration projects can ______ succeed in reintroducing native fish, coral biodiversity will recover within a decade.

(A) consistently (B) relatively (C) ridiculously (D) artificially

答案：A

詳解：副詞考查。(A) 持續穩定地、一貫地；(B) 相對地；(C) 荒誕地；(D) 人工造作地。句意指若復育計畫能持續穩定地成功引進原生魚種，故選 (A)。

Q16. It took a team of professional technicians only three days to ______ all the prefabricated concrete parts on the construction site.

(A) flex (B) dream (C) assemble (D) suffer

答案：C

詳解：動詞考查。(A) 收縮；(B) 做夢；(C) 組裝；(D) 遭受。受詞為 "all the prefabricated concrete parts"（預製混凝土構件），最適當的動詞為 assemble（組裝），故選 (C)。

Q17. Whenever my grandmother encounters technical difficulties with her new smartphone, my brother is always willing to give her a ______.

(A) hand (B) limb (C) reef (D) muscle

答案：A

詳解：片語題。"give sb a hand" 為英語常用慣用語，意為「伸出援手、提供協助」，故選 (A)。

Q18. A luxury sports car of that ultra-rare vintage would easily cost you an arm and a ______.

(A) hand (B) finger (C) leg (D) foot

答案：C

詳解：慣用語考查。"cost (sb) an arm and a leg" 為固定習語，表示「索價不斐、價值極其昂貴」，故選 (C)。

Q19. Many small coastal communities in the Pacific have ______ severely from frequent typhoons and rising sea levels over the past few years.

(A) collaborated (B) suffered (C) launched (D) assembled

答案：B

詳解：動詞考查。(A) 合作；(B) 遭受、受苦；(C) 發起；(D) 組裝。"suffer from + 災難/疾病" 表示蒙受損害或痛苦，故選 (B)。

Q20. It ______ that our principal will announce significant reforms regarding campus digital devices during tomorrow's morning assembly.

(A) seems (B) considers (C) makes (D) launches

答案：A

詳解：虛主詞句型考查。"It seems/appears that + S + V" 表「似乎……；據推測……」，為固定句構，故選 (A)。

Q21. The company managed to reduce overall operational expenses and speed ______ production by replacing human assembly with smart robotics.

(A) on (B) up (C) out (D) off

答案：B

詳解：介系詞片語考查。"speed up" 為動詞片語，意為「加速、加快步調」，受詞為 production（生產），故選 (B)。

Q22. The new athletic jacket has an extraordinarily smooth ______ that feels almost like liquid silk against the skin.

(A) sensor (B) texture (C) layer (D) degree

答案：B

詳解：名詞考查。(A) 感測器；(B) 質地、觸感；(C) 層次；(D) 程度。後文指出像絲綢般觸感，可知前文描述的是表面質地（texture），故選 (B)。

Q23. The 3D printing architectural project ______ relies on local recycled plastics has recently been approved by the municipal planning board.

(A) who (B) whom (C) which (D) what

答案：C

詳解：關係代名詞考查。先行詞為事物 "The 3D printing architectural project"，關係子句中缺少主詞，且為限定用法，應使用 which 或 that，故選 (C)。

Q24. During the heavy winter storm, traffic across the entire metropolitan area was delayed ______ the slippery frozen road conditions.

(A) because of (B) as a result (C) in order to (D) in spite

答案：A

詳解：轉折連接詞考查。空格後方為名詞片語 "the slippery frozen road conditions"，引導原因的介系詞片語為 "because of"；"as a result" 為副詞片語不能直接接名詞受詞。故選 (A)。

Q25. Many deep-sea exploration teams believe that the mysterious trenches underneath the ocean ______ still conceal hundreds of undiscovered species.

(A) layer (B) surface (C) degree (D) arm

答案：B

詳解：名詞題。(A) 層；(B) 表面；(C) 程度；(D) 手臂。"underneath the ocean surface" 表「在海平面下方」，為課文自然語境搭配，故選 (B)。

Q26. With years of dedicated study, Dr. Watson has become a world-renowned authority in the ______ of regenerative medicine.

(A) field (B) piece (C) limit (D) reality

答案：A

詳解：名詞考查。(A) 領域、田野；(B) 碎片、件；(C) 限制；(D) 現實。"in the field of..." 為常見固定語塊，表「在……領域中」，故選 (A)。

Q27. Unlike traditional handmade molds, digital printers can build objects by depositing consecutive ______ of liquid resin or powdered metal.

(A) habits (B) layers (C) limbs (D) reefs

答案：B

詳解：名詞考查。(A) 習慣；(B) 層；(C) 肢體；(D) 礁石。3D 列印的基本製造原理為「逐層沉積（consecutive layers）」，故選 (B)。

Q28. The elderly author was grateful to the nursing staff who cared for him in ways ______ he had never thought possible during his prolonged hospitalization.

(A) that (B) what (C) whose (D) where

答案：A

詳解：關係代名詞考查。先行詞為 "ways"，後方引導形容詞子句修飾先行詞，常搭配關係代名詞 that 或省略關係代名詞；what 不可作為關係代名詞接先行詞。故選 (A)。

Q29. The newly established sanctuary was specifically designed to provide a secure environment where endangered animals can breed ______.

(A) ridiculously (B) safely (C) alarmingly (D) artificially

答案：B

詳解：副詞修飾考查。(A) 荒唐地；(B) 安全地；(C) 令人警惕地；(D) 人工地。保護區（sanctuary）成立宗旨是為了讓瀕危動物安全繁衍，故選 (B)。

Q30. After Alex received his new electronic limb, he was finally able to use ______ arms to embrace his family, bringing tears of joy to everyone present.

(A) either (B) both (C) neither (D) each

答案：B

單選練習題（Q1～Q30）
Q1. The new robotic arm is capable of ______ complex surgical procedures with precision.
(A) perform　(B) performing　(C) performed　(D) to perform
答案：B
詳解：be capable of 的 of 是介系詞，後面接名詞或動名詞，故選 performing。

Q2. Many people consider ______ important to set goals for their personal and professional lives.
(A) that　(B) this　(C) it　(D) them
答案：C
詳解：句型為 S + consider + it + adj. + to VR。it 是虛受詞，真正的受詞是後面的不定詞片語 to set goals...。

Q3. The mortgage rate at this bank is lower than ______ of the other bank.
(A) it　(B) those　(C) one　(D) that
答案：D
詳解：比較的對象是單數名詞 the mortgage rate，用指示代名詞 that 代替。代替複數名詞才用 those。

Q4. AI tools can instantly carry out complex tasks, ______ enables people to work more efficiently.
(A) that　(B) which　(C) what　(D) it
答案：B
詳解：逗號後的非限定用法只能用 which，此處 which 指前面整句。that 不能用於非限定用法；what 不能引導形容詞子句；用 it 會造成兩個子句之間缺少連接詞。

Q5. The sensor in the plastic electronic arm would enable Alex ______ his hand by slightly flexing his biceps muscle.
(A) open　(B) opening　(C) to open　(D) opened
答案：C
詳解：enable + O + to VR 意為「使……能夠……」，故選 to open。

Q6. In the subtitle "Body Parts That Don't Cost an Arm and a Leg," what does the phrase mean?
(A) The body parts are not very expensive.
(B) The body parts cannot replace real arms and legs.
(C) The body parts are made without plastic.
(D) The body parts are only for children.
答案：A
詳解：cost an arm and a leg 意為「非常昂貴」，加上否定就是「不貴」。這呼應課文的 artificial limbs can be produced relatively cheaply，同時與義肢主題形成雙關。

Q7. According to the passage, why have many sea creatures suffered?
(A) Because 3D-printed reefs attract too many fish.
(B) Because the Caribbean project was launched too late.
(C) Because coral reefs are too rough in texture.
(D) Because of the decreasing number of coral reefs caused by rising sea temperatures.

Q8. Sea temperatures have risen ______ an alarming degree because of global warming.
(A) in　(B) at　(C) to　(D) for
答案：C
詳解：to a(n) + adj. + degree/extent 意為「到……的程度」，故選 to。

Q9. To help protect sea creatures' habitats, a project ______ on 3D printing has been launched.
(A) relies　(B) relying　(C) relied　(D) is relying
答案：B
詳解：句中已有主要動詞 has been launched，此處需要分詞片語修飾 project。which relies on 省略關代後，主動語意改為現在分詞 relying。

Q10. According to the notes on the 3D-printed building, how long did it take workers to assemble the concrete pieces?
(A) 2 days　(B) 17 days　(C) 250 days　(D) 19 days
答案：A
詳解：圖像筆記寫明 It took workers only 2 days to assemble these individual concrete pieces。17 天是 3D 印表機列印部件的時間，250 是建築面積（平方公尺）。

Q11. I can't imagine ______ in a world without modern technology and the internet.
(A) live　(B) to live　(C) living　(D) lived
答案：C
詳解：imagine 後面接名詞或動名詞，不接不定詞，故選 living。

Q12. ______ years of experience, Linda became an expert in the field of environmental science.
(A) With　(B) For　(C) Despite　(D) Without
答案：A
詳解：with 在此意為「憑藉」，表示 Linda 靠多年經驗成為專家。Despite（儘管）與 Without（沒有）都與句意矛盾。

Q13. Which phrase is closest in meaning to "make use of"?
(A) take part in　(B) take advantage of　(C) take care of　(D) take place
答案：B
詳解：make use of 意為「利用、運用」，與 take advantage of 同義。take part in 意為「參加」，take care of 意為「照顧」，take place 意為「發生」。

Q14. "It seems that Ben will never finish his project on time." This sentence means "Ben seems ______ his project on time."
(A) never to finish　(B) to never finishing　(C) never finishing　(D) never finished
答案：A
詳解：It seems that + S + V 可改寫為 S + seem(s) + to VR，否定詞 never 放在 to 前，故為 never to finish。

Q15. Karen solved the complex problem ______ an unexpected way, which surprised everyone.
(A) at　(B) on　(C) by　(D) in
