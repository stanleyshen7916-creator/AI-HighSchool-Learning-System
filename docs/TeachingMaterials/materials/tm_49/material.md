Q1. The volunteers worked day and night to ease the ______ of the flood victims.
(A) misery　(B) theft　(C) distribution　(D) civilization
答案：A
詳解：misery 意為「悲慘；慘況」，符合災民的處境。theft 是偷竊，distribution 是分配，civilization 是文明，三者語意都不合。

Q2. Glasses and contact lenses ______ nearsighted people to see things clearly.
(A) make　(B) let　(C) enable　(D) have
答案：C
詳解：空格後接 to see，要用 enable sb. to V（使某人能夠）。make、let、have 是使役動詞，後面接原形動詞，不接 to V。

Q3. By the time Epimetheus got to human beings, he ______ out of gifts.
(A) runs　(B) has run　(C) will have run　(D) had run
答案：D
詳解：by the time 子句用過去式 got 時，主要子句要用過去完成式 had p.p.，表示在那個時間點之前已經完成。

Q4. By the time you ______ home tonight, I ______ dinner.
(A) will get; will cook　(B) get; will have cooked　(C) got; will have cooked　(D) get; had cooked
答案：B
詳解：by the time 子句用現在式表示未來，主要子句用未來完成式 will have p.p.。時間副詞子句不可用 will，所以 A 錯。

Q5. ______ about being tricked, Zeus took fire away from humankind.

答案：C

Q6. Not only the fans but also the singer ______ immersed in the music.
(A) were　(B) was　(C) are　(D) have been
答案：B

Q7. You'd better turn your cellphone off ______ its sounds won't disturb others.

答案：A
詳解：固定片語 fall victim to + sth./sb.，意為「受……之害」，to 是介系詞。事件發生在過去，所以用 fell。

Q11. Some kinds of bats ______ insects, which helps farmers with pest control.
(A) feed with　(B) feed on　(C) feed up　(D) feed for
答案：B
詳解：feed on 意為「以……為食」，同義片語為 live on。

Q12. "The gift of fire set the stage for human civilization." The phrase "set the stage for" is closest in meaning to ______.
(A) put an end to　(B) delayed　(C) prepared the way for　(D) replaced
答案：C
詳解：set the stage for 字面上是「搭建舞臺」，引申為「為……鋪路、作好準備」。

Q13. Amy was wrongly punished, so her mom bought her new shoes to ______ it.
(A) make up with　(B) make out　(C) make up of　(D) make up for
答案：D
詳解：make up for 意為「彌補」，後接名詞或動名詞。make up with 是「與某人和好」，make out 是「理解；辨認」，都不符語意。

Q14. When the bus ran ______ gas, a friendly truck driver offered everybody a ride.
(A) out of　(B) out　(C) away from　(D) off
答案：A
詳解：主詞 the bus 後面有受詞 gas，要用 run out of + sth.。run out 單用時不接受詞，句型是 sth. runs out。

Q15. Katie is enjoying her favorite singer's latest album with her eyes ______.
(A) closing　(B) close　(C) closed　(D) to close
答案：C
詳解：with + O + OC 表示附帶狀態。眼睛是「被閉上」的，O 與 OC 是被動關係，所以用過去分詞 closed。

Q16. At that time, a Titan ______ as Prometheus created human beings out of clay.
(A) knowing　(B) known　(C) was known　(D) who known
答案：B
詳解：這是由形容詞子句 who was known as... 省略 who was 而成的分詞片語，意為「被稱作……的」。

Q17. At a time ______ there were all-powerful gods in the universe, Prometheus created humans.
(A) which　(B) where　(C) when　(D) what
答案：C
詳解：先行詞 a time 表示時間，要用關係副詞 when 引導形容詞子句。

Q18. Through his sacrifice, Prometheus came to ______ as the "Champion of Humankind."
(A) be known　(B) know　(C) being known　(D) known
答案：A
詳解：come to VR 表示「逐漸……」。Prometheus 是「被」稱為，要用被動式原形 be known。

Q19. According to the passage, why did Epimetheus have no gifts left for human beings?
(A) Zeus forbade him to give any.　(B) He had given all the gifts to animals.　(C) Prometheus had taken them away.　(D) Humans refused the gifts.
答案：B
詳解：課文寫 Epimetheus 把 speed、strength、fur、wings 給了動物，by the time he got to human beings, he had run out of gifts。

Q20. Which of the following was NOT something Prometheus gave or did for humans?
(A) The ability to stand upright　(B) Fire　(C) Wings　(D) A body made out of clay
答案：C
詳解：wings 是 Epimetheus 給動物的特質。Prometheus 用黏土造人，讓人類直立，也給了火。

Q21. Where did the gods and humans meet to decide on the distribution of sacrifices?
(A) On Mount Olympus　(B) At Mecone　(C) In a cave　(D) In a place far to the east
答案：B
詳解：課文第二段寫 the gods and humans met at Mecone。東方是 Prometheus 被放逐的地方，洞穴是人類失去火後的住處。

Q22. Why did Zeus take fire away from humankind?
(A) Humans had burned down his temple.　(B) Epimetheus asked him to.　(C) The gods needed the fire.　(D) He was angry about being tricked.
答案：D
詳解：第三段開頭寫 Angry about being tricked, Zeus took fire away from humankind，原因是在 Mecone 被 Prometheus 欺騙。

Q23. According to Paragraph 3, where did Prometheus get the fire that he gave to humans?
(A) From the sun　(B) From Zeus's palace　(C) From a volcano　(D) From Epimetheus
答案：A
詳解：課文寫 fire that he had stolen from the sun。

Q24. What did Prometheus use to carry the fire to humans?
(A) A bull's stomach　(B) A torch of olive wood　(C) A clay pot　(D) A hollow stalk of fennel
答案：D
詳解：課文寫 He took a stalk of fennel and lit its hollow center with fire。茴香莖中空，可以藏火。牛胃是 Mecone 祭品裡的道具，不是運火工具。

Q25. Which of the following about Prometheus's punishment is true?
(A) An eagle ate his heart only once.　(B) He was chained to a rock in the west.　(C) His liver kept growing back, and the eagle returned every day.　(D) Zeus forgave him a year later.
答案：C
詳解：課文寫 Though his liver kept growing back, the eagle would return every day to gnaw at it again。被啄的是肝（liver）不是心；地點在東方；課文說折磨會持續到 the end of time，沒有被原諒。

Q26. What is the main idea of the passage?
(A) Prometheus sacrificed himself to help humans and became known as their champion.　(B) Zeus was the wisest of all the gods.　(C) Epimetheus created all living creatures.　(D) Humans learned to hunt wild beasts.
答案：A
詳解：全文以 Prometheus 為人類付出、受罰，最後獲得 "Champion of Humankind" 稱號為主軸。其餘選項是錯誤細節或與課文不符。

Q27. What is the writer's attitude toward Prometheus?
(A) Critical　(B) Sympathetic and admiring　(C) Indifferent　(D) Mocking
答案：B
詳解：課文用 kind、generous 形容他，並以「人類的捍衛者」作結，態度是同情與讚揚。

Q28. "From that day on, humans would keep the meat for themselves." The phrase "from that day on" means ______.
(A) until that day　(B) on that day only　(C) a day earlier　(D) starting from that day
答案：D
詳解：from...on 表示「從……開始」，例如 from now on、from then on。

Q29. ______ his liver kept growing back, the eagle would return every day to gnaw at it again.
(A) Because　(B) Though　(C) Unless　(D) So
答案：B
詳解：though/although 引導讓步子句，意為「儘管」。這裡依課文原句作答。D 的 So 是對等連接詞，不能放在句首引導從屬子句。

Q30. In the passage, fire best symbolizes ______.
(A) punishment　(B) wealth　(C) divine anger　(D) the foundation of human civilization
答案：D
詳解：課文直接寫 the gift of fire, which set the stage for human civilization，所以火象徵文明的基礎。
