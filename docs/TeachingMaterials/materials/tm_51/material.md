Q1. We still have ______ minutes before the movie starts.
(A) few　(B) a little　(C) a few　(D) little
答案：C

Q2. The village has ______ shops or restaurants, so the residents have to drive to town for almost everything.
(A) few　(B) a few　(C) many　(D) a lot
答案：A
詳解：後句說居民幾乎什麼都得開車進城，可知村裡「幾乎沒有」商店，用 few 表否定。

答案：D

Q7. ______ I was about to read the message, my boyfriend deleted it, so I had no idea what he sent.

答案：A
詳解：as 作連接詞意為「當……時」，可換成 when，與課文 as I was leaving the hospital 同一用法。Because of 是介系詞片語，不能接子句。

Q8. After ______ the house, Allen sat down and enjoyed a cup of hot coffee in the living room.
(A) clean　(B) cleaned　(C) cleaning　(D) to clean
答案：C
詳解：After cleaning... 是由 After he cleaned... 簡化而成的分詞構句，介系詞 after 後接動名詞。

Q9. The little girl clapped her hands happily when she saw a teddy bear ______ under the Christmas tree.
(A) hide　(B) hiding　(C) to hide　(D) hidden
答案：D
詳解：泰迪熊是「被藏」在樹下，感官動詞 see + O + p.p. 表受詞被動，故選 hidden。

Q10. While trying to focus on my report, I could hear my roommates ______ about Animal Crossing very loudly.
(A) chatting　(B) chatted　(C) to chat　(D) be chatting
答案：A
詳解：感官動詞 hear + O + V-ing 強調受詞「正在」做某事。感官動詞後不接 to VR。

Q11. "Ronald," the frail voice called, ______ me to push open the door and go in.
(A) prompted　(B) prompting　(C) prompts　(D) to prompting
答案：B
詳解：句中已有主要動詞 called，逗號後用分詞構句表示主動的結果，故選 prompting。prompt sb. to VR 意為「促使某人做……」。

Q12. It was impossible ______ Duncan ______ up at 6 a.m. and join the group discussion on time.
(A) of; get　(B) for; getting　(C) for; to get　(D) to; get
答案：C
詳解：句型為 It is adj. for sb. to VR，it 是虛主詞，真正的主詞是 for Duncan to get up...。

Q13. Amy's boss didn't give her clear instructions, so she didn't know ______ she could delete the files.
(A) that　(B) what　(C) which　(D) whether
答案：D
詳解：句意為「不知道是否可以刪除檔案」，用 whether（或 if）引導名詞子句。

Q14. After the scandal was revealed, the politician had no choice ______ admit what he had done.
(A) but to　(B) except　(C) only　(D) instead of
答案：A
詳解：have no choice but to VR 意為「別無選擇只能……」。

Q15. David posted false information online, which was ______ the community guidelines of the platform.
(A) for　(B) against　(C) with　(D) toward
答案：B
詳解：against + sth. 意為「違反某事」，與課文 it was against the rules 同一用法。

Q16. Taipei 101 is ______ the tallest building in the world.
(A) not longer　(B) no more longer　(C) no longer　(D) any longer
答案：C
詳解：no longer 意為「不再」，放在 be 動詞之後。any longer 須搭配 not 並置於句尾；(A)、(B) 都是錯誤用法。

Q17. When Tony was caught ______ on the final exam, he was extremely ashamed.
(A) cheat　(B) to cheat　(C) cheated　(D) cheating
答案：D
詳解：be caught + V-ing 意為「被當場抓到正在做某事」，與課文 caught breaking the rules 同一用法。

Q18. When Tina finally arrived at the music hall, the performance by her favorite band ______.
(A) ends　(B) has ended　(C) had ended　(D) was ending
答案：C
詳解：表演結束發生在 Tina 抵達「之前」，比過去式更早的動作用過去完成式 had + p.p.。

Q19. My father always tells me ______ too much time on extracurricular activities.
(A) not to spend　(B) to not spending　(C) don't spend　(D) not spending
答案：A
詳解：tell sb. not to VR 意為「告訴某人不要做某事」，否定詞 not 放在 to 之前。

Q20. According to the "be of + abstract noun" rule, "a necklace of value" means ______.
(A) a cheap necklace　(B) a valuable necklace　(C) a necklace for sale　(D) a necklace with a price tag
答案：B
詳解：be of + 抽象名詞可換成對應的形容詞：of value = valuable，正如課文的 be of assistance = be helpful。

Q21. To thank the nanny for ______ her son on weekdays, Judy bought a bouquet of flowers for her.
(A) take care of　(B) taking care of　(C) to take care of　(D) took care of
答案：B
詳解：thank sb. for + V-ing 意為「感謝某人做了某事」，介系詞 for 後接動名詞。

Q22. Nicole didn't sleep well last night. No wonder she feels so ______ today.
(A) drowsy　(B) rewarding　(C) terminal　(D) tremendous
答案：A
詳解：沒睡好，所以覺得昏昏欲睡，feel drowsy。課文也有 he was getting drowsy。

Q23. Although Sabrina's cancer is ______, she is still very optimistic.
(A) prompt　(B) heartfelt　(C) terminal　(D) drowsy
答案：C
詳解：terminal cancer 意為「末期癌症」。Although 表示讓步：雖然是末期，她仍然很樂觀。

Q24. Sam asked Ada out on a date, but she ______ because she was already in a relationship.
(A) turned down him　(B) turned him down　(C) turned him up　(D) turned up him
答案：B
詳解：turn down 是可分式片語，受詞為代名詞時必須放在 turn 與 down 之間。turn up 意為「出現；調高」，語意不符。

Q25. According to the passage, why was Ronald not allowed to visit the children alone?
(A) He might pass on germs to them.
(B) He might frighten them with his painted face and red wig.
(C) The children's parents had complained about him.
(D) The corporation wanted to save money.
答案：B
詳解：P2 指出 never to visit the children alone..., for I might frighten them with my painted face and red wig。傳染細菌（A）是「不可有肢體接觸」的理由，不要混淆。

Q26. Why was Ronald not permitted to have any physical contact with the children?
(A) He might frighten them.
(B) He was too busy to stay long.
(C) The doctors didn't like clowns.
(D) Doing so might pass on germs to them.
答案：D
詳解：P2 指出 since doing so might pass on germs to them。

Q27. What did Ronald do first to cheer little Billy up?
(A) He colored pictures with him.
(B) He gave him a hug.
(C) He did some magic tricks for him.
(D) He sang songs for him.
答案：C
詳解：P3 寫明 I stayed and did some magic tricks for him。著色（A）是他拒絕擁抱後提議的替代活動，擁抱（B）則發生在更後面。

Q28. What made Ronald finally decide to break the rules?
(A) The look of hope in Billy's eyes.
(B) Billy's parents asked him to.
(C) His supervisor gave him permission.
(D) Billy cried loudly.
答案：A
詳解：P4 指出 I was about to refuse again, but then I saw the look of hope in his eyes，他因此不再在乎是否違規。

Q29. According to Billy's mom, what had Billy said to her?
(A) He wanted to see Ronald again.
(B) He was afraid of Ronald's red wig.
(C) He hoped to see Santa before Christmas.
(D) He didn't mind not seeing Santa because he had hugged Ronald.
答案：D
詳解：P6 引述 "Mommy, I don't mind if I can't see Santa this year because I've hugged Ronald."

Q30. What does the narrator mean by "Some things in life, after all, were more important"?
(A) Rules are always useless.
(B) He wanted to quit his job.
(C) The hospital should remove all its rules.
(D) Showing love and comfort to a sick child mattered more than strictly following the rules.
答案：D
詳解：敘述者在 P2 就表示理解規定背後的道理，並非認為規則無用（A、C 錯）。他的意思是，在那一刻，給病重的 Billy 一個擁抱、傳達愛與溫暖，比嚴守規定更重要。
